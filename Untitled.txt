Step 7: Order Tracking & Customer Portal (The Cheap Way)
- Building a custom dashboard for order tracking requires a database (Supabase/Firebase), user authentication, and heavy API work. Skip this.
- Instead, use Stripe Customer Portal (which is completely free):
- Go to your Stripe Dashboard -> Settings -> Customer Portal.
- Enable it so customers can view their past orders, download invoices, and see tracking info.
- Add a link in your Nav.astro or footer: href="https://billing.stripe.com/p/login/YOUR_PORTAL_LINK".
- When you fulfill orders, you can add tracking numbers directly inside the Stripe Dashboard. Stripe will automatically email the tracking updates to the customer.
