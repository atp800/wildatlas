1. Rainforest Trust - Protects threatened rainforests and other important ecosystems through local conservation organisations and Indigenous communities.

2. World Land Trust - Works with local partners to purchase threatened land, establish protected reserves, restore forests, and maintain habitats.

3. Wildlife Conservation Network - Funds frontline conservation organisations working with elephants, rhinos, lions, pangolins, and other threatened wildlife.

4. Woodland Trust - Protecting and restoring British woodland

5. International Rhino Foundation - Anti-poaching, rhino population recovery, and habitat management

6. Blue Marine Foundation - Protecting marine wildlife, fighting overfishing, and restoring marine ecosystems

7. Wetlands International - Wetland, peatland, and mangrove conservation; especially promising for biodiversity

8. Seawilding - Restores marine habitats by transplanting seagrass and reintroducing native oysters.

9. Coral Restoration Foundation - Grows threatened coral species in underwater nurseries and transplants them onto damaged reefs.

10. SEE Turtles - Funds local conservation teams to patrol nesting beaches, protect eggs, and help hatchlings reach the sea.

11. Clean Air Task Force (CATF) - Works to accelerate clean energy technologies and reduce emissions through technical research, regulation, and policy.

12. Future Cleantech Architects - A nonprofit think tank addressing difficult-to-decarbonise sectors, including industrial heat, aviation, and heavy industry.

13. Freshwater Habitats Trust - Creates and restores ponds, wetlands, and freshwater habitats for threatened plants and animals.

14. Rewilding Britain - Supports projects restoring natural processes, habitats, and native wildlife across Britain.

15. Bumblebee Conservation Trust - Creates and improves wildflower habitats, supports threatened bumblebee populations, and monitors species recovery.
